# Re-Power
A small enclosure connected to your PCs front panel headers to turn on and reset your PC remotely.

## Why?
I am building this because I often stream games from my PC using Moonlight, but leaving my PC always running wastes alot of energy. This remote starter allows me to turn on my PC remotely.

## Features
- Wi-Fi Control
- RF Remote
- Optional Extra Button
- Reset Trigger

## Components
- ESP32-C3 Supermini
- 2-Channel Optocoupler 
- 433MHz RF Receiver
- 433MHz 2 Button RF Remote
- Momentary Button

## Enclosure
To design the enclosure I first sketched up one in Tinkercad to see what I was working on. The Optocoupler i screwed in with its screw holes, the ESP32 gets clamped by screws, RF Receiver is secured with a click thingy, and the lid slides in tight with about 0.2mm clearence. Then I completely redesigned it in Autodesk Fusion and added the texts to show where everything goes.
